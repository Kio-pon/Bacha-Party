## About This Repo

I was really inspired by [Ali Muhammad Asad's](https://github.com/AliMuhammadAsad) **[Grad Chronicles](https://github.com/AliMuhammadAsad/Grad-Chronicles)**, which helped so many of us at Habib. But after this nobody really compiled and updated courses for the newer batches. 

So I thought, why not keep that going?

This repo will *(Insha Allah)* cover courses across **DSSE**, **iSciM** and **AHSS**.

---

## 📂 Directory Layout

Each course folder is organized into clear subfolders based on the available materials:

| Folder | Contents |
|---|---|
| **Lectures & Slides** | Weekly lecture slides, theory presentations, and notes |
| **Labs & Practicals** | Lab sheets, code starter files, tasks, and solutions |
| **Assignments & Homeworks** | Homework sheets, practice problem sets, and guidelines |
| **Quizzes & Solutions** | Past quizzes, class tests, and solved answer keys |
| **Exams & Past Papers** | Midterm and Final exam papers and revisions |
| **Readings & Textbooks** | Course textbooks, reader packets, and weekly articles |

## 🤝 How to Contribute

If you have lecture slides, lab tasks, past quizzes, or personal notes from any current or past semester, contributions are always welcome!

**Do not worry about sorting or organizing files.** You do not need to rename files or create folders. Just download what you have from Canvas / HULMS or your drive, put it in a zip, and send it over. I will take care of all the sorting and formatting.

### You can contribute by:
1. **Microsoft Teams:** Send a `.zip` archive or drive link directly to me on Teams.
2. **Pull Request:** Fork this repository, add your files, and open a PR.
